High Ki / Low Ki

     [ PC ]  気 気 気  ·  high ki  ·  inspiration
     [ NPC ]  · · ·   ·   low ki   ·  distraction

Same body. Two states. The difference is not circumstance — it is what the thoughts are doing.

The body is a radio and the thoughts are whatever is playing on it. Speak doom to it all morning and the shoulders read it before you do: breath short, jaw set, the whole instrument tuned down. Feed it the other signal and the same lungs do more with the same air. The body reacts to thought with mechanical obedience — that is not mysticism, that is the cheapest experiment available to any person, any hour, and the owl runs it every night.

Call the two states whatever you like; the brand calls them oblivion and godly. The oblivion state is the NPC state: reactive, scrolling, offended by default, doing what the feed decides. It feels like activity and produces smoke. The godly state is the player state: non-reactive, awake, in the inner child's company, making the moves it already decided on. Affirmation is its instrument, music is its weather, and inspiration is its ordinary output. High ki is simply sustained presence with the signal you chose yourself.

"The soul is dyed the color of its thoughts." — after james allen · as a man thinketh

Note the asymmetry, because it is the whole discipline: descent is passive and ascent is chosen. Nobody falls upward into the godly state. You do not owe the feed a reaction; reaction is a purchase, and the oblivious spend it by the hour. Judgment is waste. Negativity is a subscription you keep paying after the trial ended. Cancel it and the same day has more air in it.

The owl's rule: catch the state before it catches you. One breath check on waking. One hour that belongs to the work before anything external is allowed to set the dial. The frequency never changes — 33:27 — only the reception does.
